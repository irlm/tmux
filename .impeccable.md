## Design Context

### Users
Developers using this personal tmux setup for daily terminal work across local
machines and SSH sessions.

### Brand Personality
Practical, calm, playful. The user asked for special-day ASCII art and fireworks
as a small bit of fun, including a way to replay celebrations.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the existing selectable terminal palettes and monospaced ASCII drawings.
Keep the working terminal readable and support smaller terminal sizes.

### Design Principles
- Make decorative moments easy to dismiss.
- Show automatic celebrations only once per special day; make replay explicit.
- Use local dates and leave ordinary days quiet.
- Keep art legible without emoji, Nerd Fonts, sound, or network access.
- Keep motion brief and cheap: cap frame rate and canvas size, sleep between
  frames, and stop redraws once playback finishes.
